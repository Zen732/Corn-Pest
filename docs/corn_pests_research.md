# Corn Pests in Cabadbaran City and Agusan del Norte

Literature check, 15 October 2026. No published study surveys corn pests in Cabadbaran City
or Agusan del Norte specifically. The evidence below is regional (Caraga), Mindanao and
national. A key-informant interview with the Cabadbaran City Agriculture Office or the DA
Caraga Regional Crop Protection Center (Butuan City) would be the strongest local source.

## Evidence per pest

| Pest | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Fall armyworm** (*Spodoptera frugiperda*) | DA Caraga Regional Crop Protection Center, 2024 outbreak: about 770 farmers and 1,378 ha of corn damaged across Agusan del Norte, Agusan del Sur, Butuan City and Dinagat Islands (preliminary, June 2024) [1]. DA Caraga farmer guide on fall armyworm scouting and management [2]. Caraga among 12 regions with infestations, August 2024 [3]. First detected in the Philippines in 2019 [4]. | Local, strongest |
| **Asian corn borer** (*Ostrinia furnacalis*) | The most damaging corn pest in the Philippines [5]. Top insect pest over 4 years of monthly maize plantings in southern Mindanao [6]. Asian corn borer and fall armyworm named the most damaging maize pests [7, 8]. | Strong |
| **Corn earworm** (*Helicoverpa armigera*) | One of four major corn pests among 47 insect species recorded on Philippine corn [9]. Minor pest in southern Mindanao [6]. | Medium |
| **Corn leaf aphid** (*Rhopalosiphum maidis*) | Minor pest in southern Mindanao [6]. | Medium |
| **Corn leafhopper** | Minor pest in southern Mindanao [6]. | Medium |
| **Corn seedling maggot** (*Atherigona oryzae*), **root grubs** (*Leucopholis irrorata*) | Among the four major corn pests in the 1987 review [9]. Hard to photograph: inside seedlings or underground. | Medium |
| **Thrips** | Minor pest in southern Mindanao [6]. | Weak |
| **Locusts** | Caraga listed among regions reporting locusts affecting corn, no local figures [3]. | Weak |

Fall armyworm yield impact in a Mindanao setting: larvae were found on all surveyed corn farms
in Lanao del Norte, with a 48.76% yield loss [10].

## Implications for the app

- Fall armyworm and Asian corn borer, the two current pest classes, have the strongest support.
- Corn earworm, corn leaf aphid and corn leafhopper are the best-supported additions.
- Seedling maggot and root grubs are poor fits for a photo app.
